## Should You Mention the Future Work? — Yes, All Three

Your three proposed research directions connect exceptionally well to this specific audience, and
are a natural way to end the talk on an ambitious, forward-looking note.

### 1. KD field on the lattice — **definitely include**

**Why:** This is a direct extension of Part 3 and will excite Alexandre and Sarkar most.
The punchline — that a consistent chiral KD theory on the lattice *requires* anomaly
cancellation across two sheets, which maps onto the universe/anti-universe structure — is
conceptually striking and audience-appropriate.

**How to present it (2-3 sentences):**
> "The same KD-Majorana structure that explains the CPT mirror at the Big Bang turns out
> to be the key to solving the fermion-doubling problem on the lattice. Boyle's student has
> found that chiral anomaly cancellation on the lattice requires the two-sheet structure —
> the anti-universe is not just a cosmological curiosity, it is *required* by lattice consistency.
> This opens a path to first-principles simulations of the electroweak SM."

**Who will respond:** Alexandre (lattice gauge theory, chiral fermions), Sarkar (discrete symmetries), Mavromatos (SM structure).

---

### 2. Composite Higgs from dim-0 scalars — **definitely include**

**Why:** This is one of the strongest hooks for the particle physics half of the room.
John Ellis worked on Higgs physics before its discovery; Tevong You established the SM EFT
framework for characterising post-Higgs BSM physics. The claim that the 36 dim-0 scalars
collectively produce a composite Higgs — constrained by LHC data — is exactly the kind of
audacious, testable BSM idea this group thrives on.

**How to present it (2-3 sentences):**
> "The no-fundamental-scalars prediction of CPT anomaly cancellation implies the Higgs is
> composite. The challenge is building a viable composite Higgs model from the collective
> dynamics of the 36 dim-0 fields, tightly constrained by LHC data showing no new physics
> at the TeV scale. Getting this right would unify the origin of cosmic structure and the
> origin of mass in a single framework."

**Who will respond:** John Ellis, Tevong You, Fairbairn, and potentially Marsh/Ellis on
axion-scalar connections.

---

### 3. Quadratic gravity as architect of the PPS — **definitely include**

**Why:** This is the deepest and most foundational direction, and directly engages the
quantum gravity experts (Mavromatos, Sakellariadou, Lim, Gregory). The framing as a
falsifiable competition between two mechanisms — dim-0 matter fields vs. pure quantum
gravity — is intellectually clean and scientifically honest. It also naturally brings in the
Weyl anomaly (which Mavromatos and Sarkar know well) and the cosmological constant problem.

**How to present it (2-3 sentences):**
> "There is a deeper possibility: the scale-invariant power spectrum may not require any new
> matter at all, but emerge from quadratic gravity — a renormalizable theory of quantum
> gravity — at the Big Bang. Initial results from Boyle suggest this can mimic the dim-0
> scalar effect. The two scenarios are observationally distinguishable, making this a direct
> experimental window into quantum gravity."

**Who will respond:** Mavromatos (quantum gravity), Sakellariadou (loop QG, spectral geometry), Lim (modified gravity), Gregory (GR/black holes), Sarkar (string-inspired EFTs).

## Timing: Reduce Part 2, Expand Parts 1 & 3

You are right that Parts 1 and 3 will resonate most with this audience.
Part 2 is technically the most involved but least directly connected to most people's
day-to-day research. The key insight of Part 2 is conceptually simple and elegant —
compress it to that insight, not the machinery.

## Other Modification Recommendations

### Add a slide contrasting CPT-symmetric vs. CPT-violating approaches

**Reason:** Mavromatos will immediately raise his foam-based CPT *violation* framework.
Acknowledge and contrast it upfront.

| Approach | Key idea | Observational handle |
|---|---|---|
| Boyle-Turok CPT universe | Exact CPT at Big Bang; $\mathbb{Z}_2$ anti-universe | $\Omega_K < 0$, massless lightest $\nu$, scale-inv. PPS |
| Mavromatos D-foam | CPT *violated* by spacetime foam; quantum decoherence | Neutrino anomalies, neutral meson EPR correlations |
| Standard inflation | No CPT requirement | $r < 0.036$ (BICEP/Keck), $n_s \approx 0.965$ |

bridge to Alexandre's non-Hermitian / PT-symmetric work with Bender.
